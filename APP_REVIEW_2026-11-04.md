# StandIn app review — 4 November 2026

> Update, 7 November 2026: The six confirmed issues below have been addressed in the app source, including safeguards for offline corrections and retrying newer edits. The updated Firebase permissions were deployed and verified. Full shared-rule publishing remains disabled while older app versions are in use; owners' changes are saved as drafts until a compatible rollout. A new Android bundle is still needed to deliver the app changes.

StandIn should answer three questions quickly: “What is my attendance now?”, “What do I need to reach my target?”, and “What will happen if I follow my plan?” Saved attendance and past reports must remain dependable when a user changes organizations or rules.

The requested wording change is complete in the source: the future-date sheet now uses the selected date as its heading. “Plan or holiday” is removed. Going, Half day, Partial, Not going, Leave and Holiday remain available where appropriate. The earlier simpler settings names, “Count attendance on days off” and “Count attendance on holidays,” are also retained. Actual entries still use Full.

The six issues below are confirmed in the current code. They have been documented, not fixed in this review. I recommend fixing the first three before another closed-testing release. The existing version 12 bundle has not been rebuilt and does not contain the latest wording changes.

## Confirmed issues

### 1. High priority: repeated offline corrections can stop backing up

**Example:** A day is saved and synchronized. While offline, change that day from Full to Half, then from Half to Absent. On reconnect, the server rejects the final upload with permission-denied. The latest edit remains on the device; the backup still contains the older entry. Retrying the same upload does not repair it.

The local queue combines both corrections into one upload with the original server revision as its base. Its final history entry, however, is the intermediate local correction. The server requires the final history entry to match its current saved record. This was reproduced using payloads generated by the actual local repository, sent to the local Firestore emulator. Uploading the corrections in sequence succeeds.

**Recommended fix:** preserve an uploadable revision chain, or deliberately build a validated combined correction that retains every edit and the exact server base. Include retry-after-restart, an edit during an upload, first-save-offline, legacy upgrades and two-device conflicts in regression coverage. Do not loosen ownership or history protections to make the upload pass.

Sources: [queue construction](lib/src/data/local_first_attendance_repository.dart#L107), [server validation](firestore.rules#L188), [retry UI](lib/src/features/profile/attendance_sync_status.dart#L86). Evidence: [local server reproduction](build/review-sync-probe.log).

### 2. High priority: Calendar counts days before the semester starts

**Example:** Semester dates are 16 November–15 December 2026, with Monday–Friday working. On 20 November, all five semester days have been attended. Home correctly reports **5/5 = 100%**, but November in Calendar reports **5/15 = 33.3%** because it also includes weekdays before the semester began.

Monthly actual totals use the whole calendar month. Planned totals already clip the month to the configured semester/custom range, so the two calculations use inconsistent boundaries.

**Recommended fix:** use the intersection of the displayed month and the original applicable attendance period for both actual and planned totals. Dates outside that range should be clearly outside the period. Check the start and end months of semesters, academic years and custom periods, plus historical rule changes.

Source: [monthly range](lib/src/domain/policy_engine.dart#L313). Evidence: `SEMESTER` in [calculation examples](build/review-calculation-probes.log).

### 3. High priority: the warning can say a reachable target is impossible

**Example:** May 2026 has 21 working days. On Friday 29 May, attendance is 17/21, the target is 85%, and attendance on days off is allowed. The app says the maximum is **81.0%** and the target is no longer achievable. Yet a plan to attend Saturday produces **18/21 = 85.7%** and meets the target.

The maximum-possible calculation only includes future working days. It omits permitted future visits on days off and holidays.

**Recommended fix:** include allowed extra visits in the maximum-possible calculation without increasing working days. Keep actual attendance, saved plans and theoretical possibilities separate. Advice about days a user can miss must not assume they will attend an unplanned weekend. Regression coverage should also check each leave treatment.

Source: [future capacity](lib/src/domain/policy_engine.dart#L390). Evidence: `WEEKEND` in [calculation examples](build/review-calculation-probes.log).

### 4. Medium priority: changing partial hours can leave an invalid leave amount

**Example:** Choose 4 hours attendance and 4 hours leave in an 8-hour day. Increase attendance to 6 hours. The leave slider appears to show 2 hours, but the label and saved value still contain 4 hours. Save fails because attendance plus leave exceeds a full day.

This was reproduced through both the actual-attendance and future-plan controls. The slider clamps its displayed value without updating the stored selection.

**Recommended fix:** update the leave selection whenever attendance changes so the displayed value, label and saved value agree. A simple “Add leave for the remaining hours” control would also make the default sheet easier to use.

Sources: [actual attendance controls](lib/src/app.dart#L1809), [plan controls](lib/src/app.dart#L2204). Evidence: `PARTIAL` in [UI examples](build/review-calculation-probes.log).

### 5. Medium priority: half days are rounded away in the totals label

**Example:** A half-day entry produces the correct **50%**, but its accompanying text says **“1 of 1 working day so far.”** The stored calculation still contains 0.5; only the displayed count is rounded incorrectly.

**Recommended fix:** display fractional days and periods accurately, such as “0.5 of 1 day,” and omit unnecessary decimal zeros for whole values. Apply the same formatting to history and correction comparisons.

Source: [summary formatting](lib/src/domain/policy_engine.dart#L439). Evidence: `HALF DAY` in [calculation examples](build/review-calculation-probes.log).

### 6. Medium priority: Home can give an alarming heading at 100%

**Example:** On the first working day of a month, mark Full. Attendance so far is 100%, but Home says **“Attention needed.”** The heading tests whether the entire month's target has already been earned, rather than whether the user is on track so far.

**Recommended fix:** distinguish “On track so far,” “Below your target,” and “Period target reached.” Give unmarked dates their own prompt. Keep the attendance percentage and full-period forecast clearly labelled; neither heading should imply an institution has verified the result.

Source: [Home heading](lib/src/app.dart#L1005). Evidence: `HOME` in [calculation examples](build/review-calculation-probes.log).

## Improvements that support the main goal

- **Past-period reports within Calendar:** add a period selector for saved weeks, months, semesters and years, including exact date ranges and an export option. The current history page lists individual entries and previous edits; it does not provide historical period summaries. Mixed attendance units intentionally stop a combined summary, but the suggested “original attendance period” report is not available. Keep this inside the existing three-tab structure.
- **Visible backup status:** show “Saved on this device,” “Backed up” or “Needs attention,” with the last successful backup time. Currently attendance errors are mainly exposed in Profile. A user should be able to tell whether their latest tracking data will be available on a replacement phone.
- **Fewer default controls:** show the date and attendance choices first. Show leave details when needed and With plans when plans exist. Remove the non-interactive notification bell or give it a clear purpose. Retain one consistent Holiday symbol.
- **Clear owner publishing status:** full-rule publishing was disabled in the last verified backend state. The owner can currently reach Publish before receiving the disabled-feature message. Show availability before the owner edits or submits, retain their draft, and enable publishing only after the compatibility fixes and rollout checks. This review did not change that backend setting.

When implementing reports, use the saved original rules and period boundaries. Keep different units separate. An organization switch must preserve the original calendar's entries, and accepting shared updates must remain an explicit choice. Useful regression tests for these safeguards already exist; they should be extended around the failures above.
